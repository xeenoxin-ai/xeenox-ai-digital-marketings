## 1. Findings that are hurting rankings right now

### Critical

1. **Wrong category on a live citation.** promanage.biz lists the hospital under
   *Skincare, Dermatology, Aesthetics, Beauty, Facial treatments, Laser therapy,
   Acne treatment, Dermatologic surgery, Deep peelings, Skin rejuvenation* — none of
   which match a pain-management/orthopedic hospital. Wrong categories on indexed
   citations dilute the relevance signal Google matches against the actual searches
   (e.g. "pain management hospital HSR Layout"). Get this corrected or the listing
   removed.
2. **Conflicting hours across citations, and a contradiction with your own claim
   of 24/7 emergency care.**
   - promanage.biz: "12:00 AM – 12:00 PM" every day (reads as open only half the day).
   - Practo: "Mon–Sun 11:00–05:00" (ambiguous — reads like a data-entry error, not a
     real two-shift schedule).
   - Public descriptions of the hospital claim 24/7 emergency care.
   Pick the real, correct hours, set them identically on GBP and every citation, and
   make sure emergency/24-hour service is reflected accurately (Google supports a
   separate "24 hours" toggle plus specific emergency-care attributes — use it
   instead of leaving conflicting shift hours).
3. **Website domain didn't resolve.** drhealmultispecialityhospitals.com failed to
   load during this review. If this is the domain linked on your GBP profile, a dead
   link there is a direct trust and conversion killer, and Google treats an
   unreachable destination as a negative signal too. Confirm the site is live, and
   if you've moved domains, update GBP, all directories, and Facebook to the current
   URL immediately.
4. **Name-brand collision in the same neighborhood.** Two unrelated, similarly-named
   businesses also serve HSR Layout: **"Dr Heal Pharmacy"** and **"Dr Health Clinic"**
   (a skin/trichology clinic). Searchers and even directory editors can cross-list or
   confuse these. Use the exact, consistent legal/signage name ("Dr Heal Pain Cure
   Hospital" or "Dr Heal Multispeciality Hospitals" — pick one, see below) everywhere,
   and consider a short disambiguation line in your GBP description ("not affiliated
   with Dr Heal Pharmacy or Dr Health Clinic") if confusion is already costing you
   calls.

### High priority

5. **Pick one legal name and standardize it everywhere.** Right now the business
   appears as "Dr Heal Pain Cure Hospital" on some platforms and "Dr Heal
   Multispeciality Hospitals" on others. Google matches NAP across the web to
   compute trust/prominence — a name that flips between citations weakens that
   signal. Use whatever name is on your signage/registration as the single source
   of truth on GBP, then correct every directory to match it exactly.
6. **Primary/secondary category should mirror your actual differentiation.** Your
   own positioning is "non-surgical pain treatment" — that's a stronger, more
   specific relevance match for pain-related searches than the generic "Hospital"
   category alone. Recommended:
   - **Primary:** "Hospital" (if GBP won't allow "Pain management physician" as
     primary for a multi-doctor facility) — but test "Pain management physician" or
     "Orthopedic clinic" as primary if your GBP account is verified as a smaller
     clinic-type profile; whichever you use, it must genuinely match how patients
     book you.
   - **Secondary:** Orthopedic clinic, Physical therapist, Rehabilitation center,
     Emergency care service (only if genuinely 24/7).
   Compare this against Health Nest Hospital, who explicitly brand themselves "Best
   orthopedic hospital in HSR Layout" — that's the category-level relevance you're
   competing against.

## 3. Reviews

Justdial shows the review volume in the thousands for the business category page,
but that figure is aggregated across Justdial's own review system, not a confirmed
Google review count — don't quote it externally as a Google rating. Recurring
themes found in visible review snippets:

- Positive: experienced doctors and nursing staff, clean facility, affordable
  pricing for the quality delivered, courteous and patient staff.
- Negative: unresponsive front-desk staff on some visits, complaints about high
  first-visit charges, and reports of an unprofessional/rude receptionist.

**Action:** the receptionist/front-desk complaints are an operational fix as much
as an SEO one — they directly drag down the average rating that Google weighs for
prominence. Fix the service gap first; a review-acquisition push before that will
just surface more of the same complaint.
