Casement — mullion & transom

The problem

Salt air finds every weakness.

Coastal Kerala runs humid most of the year, and the monsoon drives rain sideways into every joint a window has. Timber swells, then shrinks, and stops closing square. Thin aluminium pits where the coating breaks.

The failure is rarely the glass — it is the frame. What survives is a section stiff enough not to move, protected before it is painted rather than after.

The material

Steel, because the section can be thin.

Steel carries a span at a fraction of the width aluminium or uPVC needs. That is the whole argument: more glass, less frame, and a sightline that suits both a heritage restoration and a flat modern opening.

Every section is galvanised before finishing, so the protection sits under the powder coat instead of on top of it.
